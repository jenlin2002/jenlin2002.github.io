Unit 5 住家維修

Home Repairs & AC
📍 情境核心：家裡冷氣壞掉叫修
夏天冷氣壓縮機故障、冷媒不足、技工開出分項估價單與考慮長遠換新冷氣。
1. I'm calling because my AC is not working.
我打電話來是因為我的冷氣壞了。
2. I was wondering when you guys can come out and check it.
我想詢問你們什麼時候能來檢查一下？
3. It hasn't turned on since yesterday.
它從昨天開始就沒有再啟動。
4. We'll send him in / out on Monday.
我們會安排他週一過去。
5. The (AC) unit is out here in the backyard.
（冷氣）設備在後院這邊。
6. The compressor blew out and you are also low on refrigerant.
壓縮機壞掉了，而且你的冷媒也快不足了。
7. The total before taxes is gonna be $5,325.25.
稅前價格總共是5,325.25美金。
8. I listed all these problems out on an itemized estimate / statement.
我把所有問題都列在一份有細項的估價單上了。
9. Holy moly!
天啊！
10. It's gonna run into a lot of problems.
未來可能會有很多問題。
11. I would recommend possibly looking at replacing your (AC) unit.
我建議你考慮更換（冷氣）設備。
12. In the long run.
從長遠來看。
13. I will send you this estimate for repairing your current (AC) unit.
我會把修理目前（冷氣）設備的估價單傳給你。
14. I also noticed the thermostat seems off.
我也發現恆溫器好像不正常。
15. It's not reading the temperature correctly.
因為它沒有顯示正確的溫度。
16. Thanks for stopping by!
謝謝你過來一趟！
17. Anytime / You're welcome!
不客氣！隨時為您服務！
AC Staff (客服)
Hello this is air conditioning USA. How can I help you?
哈囉，這裡是美國冷氣公司。請問我可以如何協助你？
Me (我)
Hi! I’m calling because my AC is not working. It hasn’t turned on since yesterday.
嗨！我打電話來是因為我的冷氣壞了，從昨天開始就沒再啟動。
Technician (技師)
The unit is out here. Looks like the compressor blew out and you are low on refrigerant.
設備在後院。看起來壓縮機壞了，而且冷媒不足。
Technician (技師)
The total before taxes is gonna be $5,325.25. Honestly, replacing it is better in the long run.
稅前估價是 5,325.25 美元。老實說長遠來看換新機更好。
Me (我)
Holy moly… that’s expensive! I also noticed the thermostat seems off.
天啊太貴了！我也注意到恆溫器怪怪的。
Technician (技師)
I will send you an itemized estimate with options. Thanks for stopping by! Anytime!
我會把分項估價單寄給妳。謝謝妳！不客氣！
