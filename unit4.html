Unit 4 購物與結帳

Shopping & Retail
📍 情境核心：去Forever21買衣服
進店閒逛、找試衣間試穿洋裝、詢問米色款式、無袖/長裙版型與櫃台刷卡結帳。
1. Are you looking for anything specific / particular today?
你今天有特別想找什麼嗎？
2. Just browsing / looking.
只是隨便看看。
3. Let me know if you need help with anything.
如果需要幫忙隨時告訴我。
4. Where is the fitting room?
請問試衣間在哪裡？
5. Straight down the hallway to the right.
沿著走廊直走，在右邊。
6. I'll go try it on.
我去試穿看看。
7. I was hoping it comes in beige.
我真希望有米色的款式。
8. Do you have this dress in a different color?
這件洋裝有其他顏色嗎？
9. I can help you with that.
我可以幫你看看。
10. It is available online and in store.
這款線上和門市都可以買到。
11. It comes in these colors.
有這些顏色。
12. Are you looking for a going out dress or a (more) casual dress?
妳是想找正式外出穿的洋裝還是比較休閒的洋裝？
13. Do you want a maxi dress like this one or midi or mini?
你想要像這樣的長裙，還是中長裙或短裙？
14. Sleeveless, shortsleeves, longsleeves, strapless, or no preference?
（袖子要）無袖、短袖、長袖、平肩還是沒特別偏好？
15. One is a sleeveless denim maxi with the buttons.
第一件是無袖牛仔長裙，帶有鈕扣設計。
16. Where is the register? / Where do I check out / pay?
收銀台在哪裡？
17. Are you a member with us?
您是我們的會員嗎？
18. Your total's gonna be $87.63 today.
今天的總金額是87.63美金。
19. Cash or card?
你要支付現金還是刷卡？
20. When you're ready, it'll ask you a question on the screen.
等您準備好後，螢幕上會有問題讓您回答。
21. Did you need a bag?
需要袋子嗎？
22. Do you want a copy of your receipt? / Would you like a receipt?
需要收據嗎？
Forever staff (店員)
Hi, welcome! Are you looking for anything specific today?
嗨，歡迎光臨！今天有特別想找的東西嗎？
Me (我)
No, I’m just browsing. Excuse me, where is the fitting room?
沒有，我只是隨便看看。不好意思，請問試衣間在哪裡？
Forever staff (店員)
Straight down the hall way to the right.
一直走過走廊，然後右轉。
Me (我)
Excuse me, do you have this dress in a different color? I was hoping it comes in beige.
不好意思，這件有其他顏色嗎？我真希望有米色的。
Forever staff (店員)
We’re looking at a beige strapless midi dress with a satin texture. I’ll bring it to the fitting room for you.
我們看這款米色中長平肩緞面洋裝，我幫您送去試衣間。
Me (我)
I’ll take this one. Where is the register… Ah found it.
我要這件。收銀台在哪裡……啊找到了。
Forever staff (店員)
Your total’s gonna be $87.63 today. Cash or card? Did you need a bag?
今天的總金額是87.63美元。你要付現還是刷卡？需要袋子嗎？
Me (我)
Card, and yes please. Thanks, you as well!
刷卡，要袋子，謝謝，祝您也有美好一天！
