Unit 3 餐廳點餐

Ordering Food
📍 情境核心：去Chipotle點晚餐＋去Insomnia Cookies買點心
點選墨西哥捲餅碗（選飯、豆子、肉類配料、加酪梨醬）、刷卡結帳、以及烘焙店買一盒熱餅乾。
1. What can I get started for you today?
今天想要吃什麼？
2. Can I get a bowl?
我要一份碗裝的餐點。
3. You got it.
好的馬上做。
4. What kind of rice / beans / protein?
你要什麼飯 / 豆子 / 蛋白質？
5. There's chicken, steak, carnitas, sofritas, and grilled veggies.
我們有雞肉、牛排、燉豬肉、素肉和烤蔬菜。
6. Can you do chicken and veggies, half and half?
可以雞肉和烤蔬菜各一半嗎？
7. Any salsa? Mild, medium, or hot?
要哪種莎莎醬？溫和、中辣還是辣？
8. Guac is extra.
酪梨醬需要額外收費。
9. Any chips and guac?
要加玉米片和酪梨醬嗎？
10. Cash or card?
付現金還是刷卡？
11. You're all set.
完成囉！
12. Have a good one / day!
祝你今天愉快！
13. You as well!
你也是！
14. Which flavor is the most popular?
哪一個口味最受歡迎？
15. Chocolate peanut butter cup and triple chocolate.
巧克力花生醬杯和三重巧克力。
16. Can I get a box of six?
我可以要六個裝一盒的嗎？
17. Let me get those packed for you.
我幫你打包。
18. Alrighty / Alright.
好的。
19. Did you need the receipt printed?
需要列印收據嗎？
20. Be out in just a second.
馬上為你送上！
Chipotle Staff (店員)
What can I get started for you today?
今天想點什麼呢？
Me (我)
Can I get a bowl? Brown rice and black beans.
我要一份碗裝的餐點，糙米飯和黑豆。
Chipotle Staff (店員)
And what kind of protein? There’s chicken, steak, carnitas, sofritas, grilled veggies…
那蛋白質呢？有雞肉、牛排、卡尼塔豬肉、素食豆腐和烤蔬菜。
Me (我)
Can you do chicken and veggies, half and half?
可以雞肉和蔬菜各一半嗎？
Chipotle Staff (店員)
I think so, yes of course! Any salsa? Mild, medium, or hot?
可以的，當然！要哪種莎莎醬？溫和、中辣還是辣？
Me (我)
The green one looks good to me. Corn, cheese, and guac.
我覺得綠色的不錯。玉米、起司和酪梨醬。
Chipotle Staff (店員)
Guac is extra. Is that okay? Your total is $18.50 today. Cash or card?
酪梨醬要額外加錢，可以嗎？今天總共是 $18.50，付現還是刷卡？
Me (我)
Card. You’re all set? You as well!
刷卡。祝你今天愉快！你也是！
