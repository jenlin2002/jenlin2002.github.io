Unit 14 工作閒聊

Water Cooler Chit-Chat
📍 情境核心：跟同事在洗手間與茶水間閒聊
聊週末帶小孩去南瓜園、看道奇隊棒球比賽、養寵物與養育孩子的甜蜜混亂。
1. Good to see you! How's your weekend?
很高興見到你！你的週末過得怎麼樣？
2. Can you believe how cold / hot it is outside today?
你敢相信今天外面有多冷 / 多熱嗎？
3. I took my kids to a pumpkin patch.
我帶我的孩子們去南瓜園玩。
4. They were not behaving well. You're doing your best.
他們不太聽話。你已經盡力了。
5. Did you catch the Dodgers game last night?
你昨晚有看道奇隊的比賽嗎？
6. I'm not a big baseball fan.
我不算是棒球迷。
7. Parenting is a blessing even if at times it's chaotic.
養育孩子是一個祝福，即使有時候會很混亂。
8. I should've just gotten another dog.
當初早知道我應該再養一隻狗的。
9. Before you know it, they'll be all grown up and out of the house.
轉眼之間，他們就長大然後搬出家裡了。
10. No kids, no stress. Enjoy it while you can!
沒有小孩就沒有壓力，趁還能享受的時候好好享受吧！
Coworker Karen (同事 Karen)
Hey! Can you believe how freezing it is outside today?
嗨！妳敢相信今天外面有多冰冷嗎？
Me (我)
I know! Did you do anything fun with your kids over the weekend?
真的！妳週末有帶孩子們去哪裡玩嗎？
Coworker Karen (同事 Karen)
Took them to a pumpkin patch, but they were acting crazy. Sometimes I think I should have gotten a dog!
帶去南瓜園，但吵翻天了。有時候真覺得當初養狗就好！
Me (我)
Haha! Parenting is a blessing even if it's chaotic. Enjoy it while you can!
哈哈！養孩子雖然混亂但也是祝福，好好享受吧！
