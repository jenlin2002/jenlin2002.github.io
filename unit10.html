Unit 10 心靈對話

Heart-to-Heart Talk
📍 情境核心：室友說想跟男友分手
客廳深夜心靈傾訴、失戀療傷安慰、價值觀不合與「天涯何處無芳草」鼓勵。
1. Is everything alright?
一切都還好嗎？
2. My boyfriend and I just broke up.
我和我男朋友剛分手了。
3. I'm still processing everything too.
我也還在沉澱這一切。
4. I'm sorry to hear that. / Take all the time you need.
我很遺憾聽到這個消息 / 給自己足夠的時間修復。
5. We were not on the same page.
我們在很多事情上都沒有共同的想法。
6. If you think it was the right thing to do, it's for the better.
如果你認為這是正確的決定，這樣對未來比較好。
7. There are plenty of fish in the sea.
天涯何處無芳草。（機會還很多）
8. Get out and mingle!
出去和人交際吧！
9. The silver lining is that it gives you time to focus on yourself.
好處是這讓你有時間專注在自己身上。
10. Sometimes endings are blessings in disguise.
有時候結束其實是化妝的祝福。
11. She'll come out of it.
她會走出來的。（從悲傷中恢復）
12. Is there anything I can do for you? Maybe movie night tonight?
有什麼我可以幫忙的嗎？要不要今晚來個電影之夜？
Me (我)
Hey Daisy! Why are you so quiet today? Is everything alright?
嘿 Daisy！今天怎麼這麼安靜？還好嗎？
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
My boyfriend and I broke up. We were just not on the same page anymore.
我和男友分手了。我們很多想法已經不在同一個頻率上了。
Me (我)
Take your time to heal. Plus, there are plenty of fish in the sea! It's a blessing in disguise.
慢慢療傷，天涯何處無芳草！有時候結束是化妝的祝福。
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
Thanks, girl! Maybe movie night tonight with popcorn?
謝謝妳！今晚要不要吃爆米花看電影？
Me (我)
I'll get the popcorn ready! You're the best!
我來準備爆米花！妳最棒了！
