Unit 6 旅行與住宿

Travel & Accommodation
📍 情境核心：第一次到洛杉磯機場＋旅館 check in
入境海關問答（目的、天數、住處地址、現金）、飯店櫃台辦理入住、海景套房升級與房卡。
1. What are you here for?
你來這裡的目的是什麼？
2. I'm here for a vacation.
我來這裡渡假。
3. Where are you staying?
你要住在哪裡？
4. I'm staying at a hotel for two nights then staying with my old coworker.
我會先住兩晚飯店，然後住在我的老同事家。
5. The address of the hotel is 216 Los Angeles Blvd.
飯店的地址是洛杉磯大道216號。
6. How long do you plan to stay here?
你預計在這裡待多久？
7. In total 32 days.
總共32天。
8. How much money are you bringing in cash?
你帶了多少現金？
9. A little under $2,000.
不到 2,000 美金。
10. No need to declare it.
不需要申報。
11. What is your profession?
你的職業是什麼？
12. I am a marketing manager for a tech company.
我是科技公司的行銷主管。
13. You're all set!
都好了！
14. Can I see the credit card that you used for your reservation?
我可以看一下您用來預訂的信用卡嗎？
15. I was wondering if you have any suite upgrades available today?
我想問一下今天有套房升級的選項嗎？
16. I upgraded you to an ocean view suite on the 33rd floor with a Jacuzzi.
我已經幫您升級到33樓有按摩浴缸的海景套房。
17. Did you need one or two key cards?
你需要一張還是兩張房卡？
18. What time does it open and close?
它幾點開放和關閉？（營業時間）
19. There is a $20 per day resort fee.
每天有20美金的度假村費用。
20. Enjoy your vacation!
祝您旅途愉快！
Custom (海關官員)
What are you here for? Where are you staying?
你來這裡的目的是什麼？你要住在哪裡？
Me (我)
I’m here for a vacation. I’m staying at a hotel for two nights then staying with my old coworker.
我來這裡度假，我會先住兩晚酒店，然後住老同事家。
Custom (海關官員)
How much money are you bringing in cash? And what is your profession?
你帶了多少現金？您的職業是什麼？
Me (我)
A little under $2,000. I am a marketing manager for a tech company.
不到2000美元。我是科技公司的行銷主管。
Hotel front desk (櫃台經理)
Hi, checking in? So I upgraded you to an ocean view suite on the 33rd floor with a Jacuzzi!
您好，辦理入住嗎？我幫您升級到33樓帶按摩浴缸的海景套房！
Me (我)
Oh, awesome! Two key cards please, and what time does the gym close?
太棒了！請給我兩張房卡，還有健身房幾點關閉？
Hotel front desk (櫃台經理)
It opens daily from 6 in the morning til midnight. Enjoy your vacation!
每天早上6點到午夜12點開放。祝您旅途愉快！
