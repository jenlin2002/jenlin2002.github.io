Unit 2 家庭對話

Family & Roommates
📍 情境核心：下課後跟室友聊天煮晚餐
下課回家討論做家事分工（洗碗、倒垃圾）、煮晚餐、水電工報修與成年後的壓力。
1. How's your day been? / How are you today?
你今天過得怎麼樣？
2. I'm a bit under the weather / unwell. / I'm not feeling well / too great.
我身體有點不太舒服。
3. I'm just gonna / going to take some Theraflu.
我打算服用一些感冒藥水。
4. I have some Theraflu left in the kitchen cabinets.
我廚房櫃子裡還有一些感冒藥水。
5. I went to the (running) track with Mia earlier.
我剛剛跟Mia去操場跑步。
6. One second / One moment / Hold on / Hold up / Wait.
等一下。
7. I met up with Charles at the lab.
我在實驗室跟 Charles碰面。
8. We studied for the final (exam).
我們有一起讀書準備期末考。
9. It's been a long day.
今天真是漫長的一天。（發生好多事很疲憊）
10. I'll help with the dirty dishes while you make dinner!
我來幫忙洗碗，妳來做飯！
11. Who is on trash duty?
今天是誰負責倒垃圾？
12. Let's just divide up the chores.
我們可以分配一下家務。
13. Take out the trash.
倒垃圾。
14. I'll make us some spinach omelette.
我來做菠菜歐姆蛋。
15. We'll have a pregame in the living room.
我們星期五出去前可以在客廳來場小酌。
16. I was planning to switch up my room.
我打算那時候重新佈置房間。
17. No biggies / No worries.
沒什麼大不了的。
18. You do you.
做自己。
19. The faucet's been acting up lately.
那水龍頭最近又出狀況了。
20. Is it leaking again?
又漏水了嗎？
21. Call the plumber.
打電話叫水電工。
22. Adulting is hard.
長大了真的不容易。
23. I feel you / I get you.
我懂你。
24. On the bright side.
往好的方面想。
25. Have a blast!
玩得開心！
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
Hey! How’s your day been?
嘿！今天過得怎麼樣？
Me (我)
Just got back from my last class. What about you?
剛從最後一堂課回來。妳呢？
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
Ugh… I think I’m a bit under the weather. Nothing too serious though. I’m just gonna take some Theraflu.
唉……我身體有點不太舒服，但應該沒什麼大礙。我打算服用一些感冒藥水。
Me (我)
Oh no, get well soon! I have some Theraflu left in the kitchen cabinets.
喔不，快點好起來！我廚房櫃子裡還有一些感冒藥水。
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
Yup, it’s been a long day. Let’s get cooking!
是啊，今天真是個漫長的一天。來一起做晚飯吧！
Me (我)
Aight. I’ll help with the dirty dishes while you make dinner! Who’s on trash duty today?
好啊。我來幫忙洗碗，妳來做飯！今天是誰負責倒垃圾？
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
Not sure but let’s just divide up the chores. You’ll do the dishes and take out the trash, and I’ll make us some spinach omelette and baked chicken.
不確定，但我們可以分配一下家務。妳洗碗和倒垃圾，我來做菠菜歐姆蛋和烤雞。
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
Also, the faucet’s been acting up lately.
還有，那水龍頭最近又出狀況了。
Me (我)
The faucet? Is it leaking again?
水龍頭？又漏水了嗎？
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
Yeah, we should call that plumber we used last time and ask him to fix it up.
對啊，我們應該打電話叫上次那個水管工來修一下。
Me (我)
Adulting is hard, Daisy!
長大了真的不容易，Daisy！
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
I feel you. On the bright side, we will have a blast at the party on Friday.
我懂你。往好的方面想，我們星期五派對會玩得很開心。
Me (我)
Can’t wait!
等不及了！
