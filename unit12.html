Unit 12 生活分享與邀約

Life Updates & Invites
📍 情境核心：跟室友的週五閒聊與邀約
聊論文進度、下班搭車塞車買外帶、朋友邀約週末去海灘爬山、禮貌委婉拒絕邀約（Take a rain check）。
1. How was work? / How was school?
工作如何？ / 學校怎麼樣？
2. I had lunch late because I was in a meeting.
我午餐吃得比較晚，因為在開會。
3. The train ran late so it took me extra long to get back.
火車延誤了，所以我花了更長的時間才回來。
4. I finally handed in my essays.
我終於交了論文報告。
5. What is your plan for this weekend?
你週末有什麼計劃嗎？
6. We're going on a hike tomorrow. Would you like to come?
我們明天要去爬山。你想一起來嗎？
7. If you're free, you should come hang with us!
如果你有空，你應該一起加入我們！
8. Thanks for inviting me but I'm going to have to take a rain check this time.
感謝你的邀請，但這次我得改天再跟你約（婉拒）。
9. I would love to but I have to get this essay turned in tomorrow.
我很想去，但我明天得交這篇論文。
10. I'm so sorry but I won't be able to make it this time. Have fun!
非常抱歉，但這次我沒辦法參加。祝你們玩得開心！
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
How was school? Did you hand in the paper?
今天上課如何？論文交了嗎？
Me (我)
Finally handed it in! But train ran late so it took forever.
終於交了！但火車大誤點花了好久才回家。
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
We are going to the beach tomorrow. Wanna join us?
我們明天要去海邊，要一起來嗎？
Me (我)
I'd love to, but I have another deadline tomorrow so I'll take a rain check!
超想去，但我明天還有另一個報告要交，這次先改天囉！
