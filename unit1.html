Unit 1 認識新朋友

Meeting New Friends
📍 情境核心：去室友的朋友跨年派對
跨年派對前在宿舍化妝準備，隨後到派對認識新朋友聊家鄉、科系與音樂愛好。
1. You're coming to the party later tonight, right?
今晚你會來派對吧？
2. Start getting ready.
開始準備（出門）。
3. I literally just landed and got no fancy outfit.
我剛下飛機，根本沒有帶正式的衣服。
4. No worries. / No biggies. / Don't worry about it. / It's okay.
別擔心。
5. Nice meeting you. / Nice to meet you.
很高興認識你。
6. My family lives 2 hours away.
我家人住在離這裡兩小時的地方。
7. We moved here 10 years ago from Virginia.
我們十年前從維吉尼亞州搬到這裡。
8. I've never been there.
我從沒去過那裡。
9. I would like to visit one day.
我希望有一天能去那裡。
10. There are a lot of museums and historical sites to visit.
那裡有很多博物館和歷史景點可以參觀。
11. I'd imagine.
我想也是。
12. Considering it's one of the oldest cities.
畢竟那是最古老的城市之一。
13. What's your major?
你的主修是什麼？
14. I am in Beasley School of Law.
我就讀於 Beasley 法學院。
15. But I'm actually more interested in making music or playing pickleball.
但我其實更喜歡製作音樂或玩匹克球。
16. I've always wanted to try pickleball.
我一直想打打看匹克球。
17. It varies. / It depends.
看情況 / 不一定。
18. I make mostly country and jazz music.
我主要是做鄉村音樂和爵士樂。
19. What brought you back to business school?
你怎麼會想回商學院？
20. Mostly I want to be an independent musician.
主要是因為我想當獨立音樂人。
21. My father encouraged me to come back to school.
我父親鼓勵我回到學校。
22. You have a wise dad.
妳有一位很有智慧的爸爸。
23. I assume / suppose you are gonna practice law?
我猜你將來會當執業律師吧？
24. I don't think that's gonna / going to happen.
我覺得應該不會。
25. You should pursue / go after it.
你應該追求那個目標。
26. Whatever it is that is true to your heart!
做妳真正熱愛的事情！
27. Go grab some sliders and alcohol.
去拿點小漢堡和酒。
28. I'll see you on the rooftop for the countdown later.
等會倒數時屋頂見喔！
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
Hey! You’re coming to the party later tonight, right?
嘿！今晚你會來派對吧？
Me (我)
Oh yeah! What time does it start?
哦，當然！派對什麼時候開始？
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
At eight!
八點！
Me (我)
Sweet. I’ll start getting ready.
太棒了！那我開始準備。
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
You can borrow my pink dress. I have two dresses and I think you will look great in the pink one!
你可以借我的粉色洋裝。我有兩件洋裝，我覺得你穿粉色那件會很好看！
Me (我)
Awww that would be great since I literally just landed and got no fancy outfit.
哇，那太好了！因為我剛下飛機，根本沒有帶正式的衣服。
Roommate Daisy (室友 Daisy)
No worries! I got you, girl. Let’s start getting ready!
不用擔心！我幫你搞定，女孩～我們一起開始準備吧！
Jessica (新朋友 Jessica)
So you’re Daisy’s new roomie, right?
所以妳是 Daisy 的新室友，對吧？
Me (我)
Yeah, hi! Nice meeting you.
對啊，嗨！很高興認識妳。
Jessica (新朋友 Jessica)
Hi, I’m Jessica. So… where are you from?
嗨，我是 Jessica。妳來自哪裡？
Me (我)
I’m from Taiwan!
我來自台灣！
Jessica (新朋友 Jessica)
Wow, that’s nice. I have a friend who’s also from Taiwan! Well, I’m from Philly, but my family lives 2 hours away and we moved here 10 years ago from Virginia.
哇，真棒！我有一個朋友也來自台灣！我來自費城，但我家人住在離這裡兩小時的地方，我們十年前從維吉尼亞搬到這裡。
Me (我)
Sweet! I’ve never been there but I would like to visit one day!
太酷了！我從沒去過那裡，但我希望有一天能去看看！
Jessica (新朋友 Jessica)
Yeah, for sure you should! There are a lot of museums and historical sites to visit.
妳一定要去！那裡有很多博物館和歷史景點可以參觀。
Me (我)
I’d imagine, considering it’s one of the oldest cities. What’s your major?
我可以想像，畢竟那是最古老的城市之一。妳主修什麼？
Jessica (新朋友 Jessica)
Nice! I’m in Fox Business School. But I’m actually more interested in making music or playing pickleball.
很棒！我在 Fox 商學院。但我其實更喜歡製作音樂或玩匹克球。
Me (我)
I’ve always wanted to try pickleball! What kind of music do you make?
我一直想試試匹克球！妳做什麼類型的音樂？
Jessica (新朋友 Jessica)
It varies. Mostly country and jazz. That’s a good thing! I’m gonna go grab some sliders and alcohol.
各種都有，主要是鄉村和爵士樂。我要去拿點迷你漢堡和酒。
Me (我)
Aight. I’ll see you on the rooftop for the countdown later!
好啊。等會倒數時屋頂見！
