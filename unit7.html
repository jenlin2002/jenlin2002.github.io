Unit 7 健康與醫療

Health & Medical Care
📍 情境核心：發燒拉肚子掛病求診
前往急診 Urgent Care 掛號填表、向醫生描述發燒脫水與疑似腸胃炎症狀、給予用藥建議。
1. What brings you here today?
今天哪裡不舒服？
2. I think I have a fever.
我覺得我發燒了。
3. I will need your (picture) id.
我需要您的身份證件。
4. Date of birth?
出生日期？
5. I also need the insurance card.
我也需要你的醫療保險卡。
6. Fill out this top portion of the form, sign, and put down the date.
請填寫表格上半部，簽名並填上日期。
7. Take a seat there and the doctor will call you.
請在那邊坐一下，醫生會叫您。
8. I've been having a fever since yesterday, diarrhea too.
我從昨天開始發燒，還有拉肚子。
9. At that point, I hadn't eaten anything for 30 hours.
當時我已經大概30個小時沒吃東西了。
10. I started getting chills.
我開始發冷。
11. Any exposure to someone with a flu?
你是否有接觸有流感的人？
12. You're negative for covid, and also negative for the flu.
你的 COVID 和流感檢測結果都是陰性的。
13. Is it associated with anything that you ate?
會不會是跟你吃的東西有關？
14. I just had some leftover pasta from a restaurant.
我只是吃了餐廳的義大利麵剩菜。
15. Are you allergic to any medications?
你有對任何藥物過敏嗎？
16. Your heart rate is a bit elevated.
你的心率有點偏高。
17. Give them a stool sample.
給他們送糞便樣本。
18. Chills and headaches are primarily caused by dehydration.
發冷和頭痛主要是因為脫水造成的。
19. Try to stay away from spicy food, greasy food, and dairy.
盡量避免辛辣食物、油膩食物和乳製品。
20. Electrolyte drinks will help you a lot. Feel better!
電解質飲料對你會有很大的幫助。希望你早日康復！
Receptionist (櫃台)
Hi, what brings you here today? I will need your photo ID and insurance card.
您好今天哪裡不舒服？我需要看您的證件和保險卡。
Me (我)
I think I have a fever and diarrhea since last night. I started getting chills.
我好像發燒拉肚子了，昨天開始發冷。
Doctor (醫生)
Good news: you're negative for COVID and flu. Did you eat any leftover food?
好消息：流感與新冠都是陰性。妳最近有吃隔夜剩菜嗎？
Me (我)
Yes, some leftover pasta. Not allergic to any medications.
有，吃了些剩義大利麵。我對藥物沒有過敏。
Doctor (醫生)
Headaches and chills are from dehydration. Drink electrolyte drinks and avoid dairy. Feel better!
頭痛發冷主要來自脫水。多喝電解質水、別吃乳製品。祝早日康復！
