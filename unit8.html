Unit 8 客訴與抱怨

Complaints & Support
📍 情境核心：租車結果輪胎壞掉
高速公路匝道前胎壓燈亮起，緊急致電租車客服尋求拖車救援、趕航班焦慮與賠償爭取。
1. Hello. How can I help you?
喂，請問有什麼可以協助您的？
2. I'm calling because the tire pressure light has been on.
我打電話是因為輪胎氣壓燈亮了。
3. I did try to put air in the tires.
我有嘗試幫輪胎打氣。
4. I didn't want to risk it on a 2-hour freeway drive.
我不想冒險開兩小時的高速公路。
5. Who am I speaking to?
請問怎麼稱呼您？
6. I apologize for the inconvenience.
很抱歉造成您的不便。
7. We will send someone to tow your car away.
我們會派人去拖您的車。
8. Where are you located? We are right down the ramp of a freeway.
您的位置在哪裡？我們就在高速公路出口匝道下方。
9. I see a McDonald's across the street.
我看到對街有一家麥當勞。
10. The tow truck is on the way.
拖車已經派遣過去了。
11. Our flight is taking off in 4 hours.
我們的航班四個小時後就要起飛了。
12. We were cutting it close.
我們時間已經很緊迫了。
13. Will the cost be compensated?
你們會補償費用嗎？
14. What would you suggest?
你建議怎麼做？
15. I might have to miss my flight.
我可能會錯過我的航班。
16. You can talk to the branch manager.
你可以和門市經理聯絡。
Customer Service (租車客服)
Elephant Car Rental support. How can I help you?
這裡是租車客服，請問有什麼可以協助您的？
Me (我)
The tire pressure light is on. I tried putting air but it's not working, and our flight leaves in 4 hours!
胎壓燈亮了，打氣也沒用，而且我們4小時後班機要起飛！
Customer Service (租車客服)
The tow truck is on the way, arriving in 20 minutes. Where are you located?
拖車已出發約20分鐘抵達。請問您的確切位置？
Me (我)
Down the freeway ramp across from McDonald's. Can you compensate for our Uber or hotel?
在高速匝道下麥當勞對面。你們可以補償我們叫Uber或住宿費嗎？
Customer Service (租車客服)
Unfortunately we cannot authorize that from support, but you can speak to the branch manager.
很遺憾線上客服無法授權，但您可以向門市經理洽談。
