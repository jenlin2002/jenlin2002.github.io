Unit 13 工作與面試

Job Interview & Career
📍 情境核心：去外商應徵行銷主管
外商行銷經理面試（自我介紹、Amazon 廣告活動經驗、換跑道動機、薪資期待與 401k 福利）。
1. You must be Susan!
你就是蘇珊吧！
2. We'll learn more about your professional experience.
我們會更深入了解你的專業經驗。
3. Can you briefly tell me more about yourself?
可以簡單介紹一下你自己嗎？
4. My main responsibilities include monitoring paid ad campaigns on Amazon.
我的主要工作包括監控亞馬遜上的付費廣告活動。
5. Can you share an example of a marketing campaign you worked on?
能分享一個你曾做的行銷活動案例嗎？
6. A Black Friday sales campaign. We hit that target.
一個黑色星期五促銷活動，我們達成了那個目標。
7. How will you add value to our company in the first 90 days if you are hired?
如果你被錄取，在前90天內如何為我們公司帶來價值？
8. What are your salary expectations?
你的期待薪資是多少？
9. Based on my last job, I am looking at a salary of $85,000.
根據我上一份工作的薪資，我希望薪資能在 85,000 美元左右。
10. Our benefits include 3 insurances: Medical, dental, and eye care.
我們的福利包括三種保險：醫療、牙科和眼部保健。
11. We also include 401k and match up employee contributions up to 3%.
我們有 401k 福利，並提供至多 3% 的員工自付對等撥提。
12. Every Tuesday, we have Lunch and Learn. We will be in touch through email.
每週二我們有「午餐與學習」活動。我們會透過 Email 與您保持聯繫。
Interviewer Susan (面試官 Susan)
Hi! Can you briefly tell me more about yourself?
嗨！可以簡單介紹一下你自己嗎？
Me (我)
For sure. In the past 3 years, I worked as a marketing manager monitoring ad campaigns on Amazon and Google.
當然，過去三年我擔任行銷經理，負責監控亞馬遜與Google的廣告活動。
Interviewer Susan (面試官 Susan)
What are your salary expectations?
最後，你的期待薪資是多少？
Me (我)
Based on my last job, I am looking at a salary of $85,000. I'm open to discussion.
根據我上一份工作的職責，我期待在 85,000 美元左右，抱持開放討論態度。
Interviewer Susan (面試官 Susan)
Everything sounds good. We will be in touch through email!
聽起來都很不錯，我們後續會通過 Email 與您聯繫！
