Unit 15 新聞時事探討

News & Current Affairs
📍 情境核心：跟室友看電視聊新聞與通膨
聊物價通膨飆升（汽油加滿變貴）、選舉民調、媒體聳動報導與華爾街房市飆漲。
1. Just a little down in the dumps.
只是有點情緒低落 / 心情沮喪。
2. Prices have been skyrocketing.
物價一直飆升。
3. It used to cost 20 dollars to fill up.
以前加滿油箱只要 20 美元。
4. I was looking at the polls.
我剛剛在看民調 / 調查問卷。
5. It will have a negative impact on the economy.
這會對經濟產生負面影響。
6. Most of it is not even objective news reporting, it's just sensationalism.
大多數根本不是客觀新聞報導，只是聳動煽情。
7. You are only bringing yourself down by reading up on this stuff.
你光是讀這些東西，只是讓自己心情更低落。
8. Look at the housing market. Prices have gone up three times.
看看房地產市場，房價都漲了三倍。
9. Caught red-handed.
當場被抓包（做壞事或尷尬時）。
10. How about we head out and grab a coffee? My treat!
我們出去喝杯咖啡怎麼樣？我請客！
Roommate Marry (室友 Marry)
I'm just so down in the dumps looking at the news and gas prices.
看新聞和油價飆漲，心情真的好沮喪。
Me (我)
Watching too much news will only make you depressed. Most of it is pure sensationalism.
看太多新聞只會讓妳憂鬱，大部分都是聳動報導。
Roommate Marry (室友 Marry)
True. You seem to know a lot about housing prices though!
也是。不過妳好像對房價也很了解嘛！
Me (我)
Oops... caught red-handed! Tell you what, let's grab coffee. My treat!
哎呀被抓包了！這樣吧，我們去買杯咖啡，我請客！
